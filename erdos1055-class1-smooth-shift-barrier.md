# Erdős #1055 — class-1 smooth-shift barrier

**Author:** Jared Wilder  
**Release:** 2026-09-11

Under the frozen classification used by the #1055 campaign, a class-1 prime is exactly a prime `p` for which

\[
\boxed{p+1=2^a3^b}
\]

for some nonnegative integers `a,b`.

Consequently, the infinitude clause for class-1 primes already contains the open problem of whether infinitely many primes occur one below a `3`-smooth integer.

## Meaning

This is a hardness / problem-identification reduction, not progress toward proving the infinitude. It isolates a classical-looking prime-value obstruction that any proposed elementary close must overcome rather than allowing the campaign to mistake easy finite data for an asymptotic theorem.

## Scope boundary

No infinitude theorem is claimed. No historical novelty claim is made for unpacking the frozen definition into the smooth-shift form.
